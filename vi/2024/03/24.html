New Aztec Codices Uncovered in San Andrés Tetepilco​

  • The discovery showcases fresh iconography and Aztec writing, clarifying previously obscure glyphs and communication methods.
  • The Mexican government now houses the codices at the National Institute of Anthropology and History, sparking enthusiasm for their future publication.

Reactions​

  • New Aztec codices were found in San Andrés Tetepilco, Mexico, acquired by the Mexican government from a local family, believed to contain previously erased Aztec text visible under multispectral imaging.
  • The discovery expands the existing collection of Aztec codices, offering fresh perspectives on Aztec history and culture, including details about San Andrés Tetepilco's establishment and the Tenochtitlan polity.
  • Researchers plan to conduct further in-depth analysis of the codices in the upcoming years, unveiling insights into pre-Aztec and post-conquest historical events.
