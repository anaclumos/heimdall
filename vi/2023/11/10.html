You Have a Right to Know: Investigating Health Insurers Denying Claims and Withholding Explanations​

  • ProPublica investigated the practice of health insurers denying claims and failing to provide patients with records explaining the reasons for denial, as required by federal regulations.
  • Some insurers only provided the records after ProPublica intervened, prompting the publication of a guide on how to request claim files and the sharing of resources with healthcare providers and advocates.
  • ProPublica found that many patients faced challenges such as missed deadlines and wrongful requirements for subpoenas, prompting five insurance companies to update their policies in response.

Reactions​

  • ProPublica investigated health insurers denying claims and failing to provide patients with records explaining the denials, in violation of federal regulations.
  • Some insurers only provided the records after ProPublica intervened, prompting them to publish a guide on how to request claim files and share resources with healthcare providers and advocates.
  • ProPublica raised the issue with five insurance companies, who acknowledged their obligation to provide the requested information and updated their policies in response.

Cars Allowed to Harvest Texts and Call Logs without Authorization, Rules Court​

  • Washington appeals court dismisses five class-action lawsuits against car manufacturers, including Honda, Toyota, Volkswagen, and General Motors, regarding storing text messages and call logs from connected smartphones.
  • The court ruled that the claims did not meet the statutory injury requirements of the Washington Privacy Act, stating that the privacy violation is considered potential rather than actual.
  • Car manufacturers are now allowed to intercept, record, download, store, and transmit text messages and call logs without authorization. Further appeals to the Supreme Court are possible.

Reactions​

  • A Washington appeals court has ruled that car manufacturers did not break privacy laws by storing text messages and call logs from connected smartphones.
